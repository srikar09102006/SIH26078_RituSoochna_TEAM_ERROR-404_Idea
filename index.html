System overview

About RituSoochna

Anomaly Understanding & Risk Analysis · Concept prototype for MOES
01

Ingest forecast fields

Bring together gridded forecast variables such as precipitation, temperature, wind, and humidity over the medium-range forecast window.

02

Detect unusual conditions

Compare forecast values with expected patterns and identify spatial clusters that cross configurable anomaly thresholds.

03

Track through time

Associate signals between forecast steps to show how an event develops, shifts location, and changes intensity.

04

Estimate confidence

Summarize ensemble agreement and signal persistence to communicate uncertainty alongside anomaly severity.

05

Surface priority events

Rank notable signals by severity and confidence, then provide concise context to support analyst review.

06

Support human review

Give forecasters a map-led interface for exploring locations, forecast steps, event details, and exported summaries.

Prototype data notice: All event locations, forecast values, confidence scores, and AI-generated summaries in this build are simulated for demonstration. The interface is not connected to operational MoES or IMD forecast services, and it must not be used for public warnings or decision-making.
